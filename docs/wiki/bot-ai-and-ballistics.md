# Bot Difficulty And Collision Reports

[简体中文](bot-ai-and-ballistics.zh-cn.md) · [Player Guide](home.md)

Practice offers four bot difficulty settings. To compare them, keep the arena, starting position and build the same and record the selected setting for each round. A single outcome cannot distinguish a difficulty change from variation between rounds.

For a collision report, record the arena, landmark, camera position and whether the visible obstacle agreed with the movement or hit result. A screenshot from each side of the same obstacle makes mismatches easier to locate. For an online report, identify which view belonged to the host. Omit room codes and account details.

The difficulty model and collision specification are tracked in [issue 13](https://github.com/BigBrainDevBros/FaceOff/issues/13). Use that issue to distinguish documented behavior from changes under discussion.
